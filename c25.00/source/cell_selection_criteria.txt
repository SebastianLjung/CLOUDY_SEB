CELL SELECTION FOR THE CLOUDY HII-REGION MODELS OF SPHINX20 HALOES
====================================================================
Source of truth: CLOUDY_TEST/c25.00/source/plot_SPHINXHIIs.py, sections "BPASS: Q(H) OF EVERY STAR" and
"CELL SELECTION" (parameters at the top of the file, "KEY PARAMETERS TO CHANGE").
Written 2026-11-06 for the settings in the file at that date. If the two disagree, the script wins.

Purpose: plot_SPHINXHIIs.py models, with Cloudy, the gas cells of a halo that hold star particles, one model per
cell. Most star-hosting cells do not need a model: the rules below decide which do. This file describes the rules
so that they can be applied while extracting a halo from the simulation, keeping only the cells (and their star
particles) that will be modelled.


1. INPUTS PER HALO (as currently extracted by extract_halo_cells.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
gas_cells.npz, one entry per AMR leaf cell of the halo:
  nH           [cm^-3]   hydrogen number density
  xHII         [-]       H ionised fraction (SPHINX)
  TK           [K]       temperature
  ne           [cm^-3]   electron density           (not used by the selection)
  metallicity  [-]       metal mass fraction        (used by the Cloudy decks, not the selection)
  xHeII, xHeIII          (not used by the selection)
  cell_pos     [box units, 0-1]  cell centre (x, y, z)
  cell_l       [-]       AMR level; cell side in box units = 0.5**cell_l
  cell_dx      [cm]      cell side (proper)
stars.npz, one entry per star particle:
  pos          [box units]  position
  star_age     [yr]
  star_met     [-]       metal mass fraction
  star_minit   [1e11 Msun]  initial mass (multiply by 1e11 for Msun)
  mass, galaxy_index     (not used)
halo_info.json: 'redshift' (used by the Cloudy decks for the CMB).


2. CONSTANTS
------------
T_ION     = 1e4 K                   temperature of ionised gas in the Stromgren estimate
Y_He      = 10**-1.103 = 0.0789     He/H by number (as in the Cloudy decks)
R_eq_cube = (3/(4 pi))**(1/3) = 0.6204   radius of the sphere with the volume of a cube of side 1
alpha_B(T) [cm^3 s^-1], case-B H recombination (Hui & Gnedin 1997):
    lam = 315614.0 / T
    alpha_B = 2.753e-14 * lam**1.5 / (1 + (lam / 2.74)**0.407)**2.242      (alpha_B(1e4 K) = 2.59e-13)
pc = 3.086e18 cm, h = 6.62607e-27 erg s, c = 2.99792458e10 cm/s, lambda_912 = 911.2671 Ang


3. PARAMETERS (current values)
------------------------------
XHII_TRUST      = 1.0           cells with x_HII >= this are trusted as ionised
N_HI_THIN       = 1e15 cm^-2    cells with N_HI_min below this are trusted as ionised
N_HI_MAX        = 3e20 cm^-2    cells with N_HI_min above this are optically thick
STROMGREN_RANGE = (0.8, 1.2)    allowed range of x_HII / x_S
Q_FRAC          = 0.98          the faintest candidate cells, together holding < (1 - Q_FRAC) of the halo's
                                stellar Q(H), are left out (Q_MIN is set per halo from this)
EXTRA_CELLS     = []            cells forced into the model list by hand (none at present; ignore)


4. STEP A: HOST CELL OF EVERY STAR (must be done with ALL the halo's cells and stars)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With AMR the nearest cell centre is not always the cell that contains a star, so the 8 nearest centres are
checked and the one whose cube contains the star is used:
    _, nearest = cKDTree(cell_pos, leafsize=100).query(star_pos, k=8)
    half_dx = 0.5 * 0.5**cell_l[nearest]                                   # box units
    inside = np.all(np.abs(star_pos[:, None, :] - cell_pos[nearest]) <= half_dx[..., None], axis=2)
    star_in_cell = inside.any(axis=1)                                      # False: star outside all extracted cells
    star_cell = nearest[np.arange(len(star_pos)), np.argmax(inside, axis=1)]
Stars with star_in_cell == False are dropped (halo 997: 525 of 702,907).


5. STEP B: Q(H) OF EVERY STAR (BPASS)
-------------------------------------
Grid: CLOUDY_TEST/c25.00/data/bpass_sphinx.ascii (Cloudy's BPASS grid; L_lambda [erg/s/Ang] per Msun on a
(Z, age) grid; ages 1e6-1e11 yr, Z 1e-5-0.04). Reader: load_bpass() in plot_SPHINXHIIs.py.
  - q(Z, age) [photons/s/Msun] = trapz over lambda <= 911.2671 Ang of L_lambda * lambda * 1e-8 / (h c)
  - interpolate log10 q linearly in (log10 Z, log10 age) (scipy RegularGridInterpolator), with
      log10 Z   clipped to [-5.00, -1.40]
      log10 age clipped to [log10 1e6, log10 1e11]   (stars younger than 1 Myr get the 1 Myr value)
  - Q_star = 10**interp(logZ, logage) * star_minit[Msun]
Per cell: Q_cell = sum of Q_star of its stars; N_star = number of stars (step A, star_in_cell only).
Q_tot = Q_cell.sum() = the halo's stellar Q(H) (halo 997: 1.50e53 s^-1).


6. STEP C: PER-CELL QUANTITIES
------------------------------
    V_cell    = cell_dx**3                                              [cm^3]
    R_out     = R_eq_cube * cell_dx                                     [cm] sphere with the cell's volume
    N_target  = nH * V_cell                                             H atoms in the cell
    N_HI_min  = nH * (1 - xHII) * R_out                                 [cm^-2] neutral column (see note 1)
    N_ion_max = sqrt(Q_cell * V_cell / ((1 + Y_He) * alpha_B(T_ION)))   H atoms the cell's stars can keep ionised
                                                                        (Stromgren: Q = alpha_B n_e n_p V, uniform)
    crit      = N_ion_max / N_target                                    > 1: stars could ionise the whole cell
    x_S       = min(crit, 1)                                            Stromgren ionised fraction of the cell
    ratio     = xHII / x_S                                              (cells without stars: x_S = 0 -> inf/nan;
                                                                         they are excluded by has_stars anyway)


7. STEP D: THE RULES, IN THIS ORDER (each cell gets exactly one category)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    has_stars     = N_star > 0
    ionised       = (xHII >= XHII_TRUST) | (N_HI_min < N_HI_THIN)          -> TRUSTED, f_esc = 1, not modelled
    thick         = N_HI_min > N_HI_MAX                                    -> THICK, f_esc = 0, not modelled
    off_stromgren = (ratio > STROMGREN_RANGE[1]) | (ratio < STROMGREN_RANGE[0])
                    ratio > 1.2: ionised beyond what the cell's own stars can do (photons from outside the cell)
                    ratio < 0.8: less ionised than the stars would make it in equilibrium
                                                                           -> OFF-STROMGREN, not modelled; f_esc
                                                                              0 or exp(-6.3e-18 N_HI_min)
    candidates    = has_stars & ~ionised & ~thick & ~off_stromgren
    # per-halo Q(H) cut: leave out the faintest candidates while their summed Q(H) stays within
    # (1 - Q_FRAC) of the WHOLE HALO's Q(H) (not of the candidates' Q(H))
    Q_sorted = np.sort(Q_cell[candidates])                                 # faintest first
    n_faint  = np.searchsorted(np.cumsum(Q_sorted), (1 - Q_FRAC) * Q_tot, side='right')
    Q_MIN    = Q_sorted[n_faint - 1] if n_faint > 0 else 0.
    faint         = Q_cell <= Q_MIN                                        -> FAINT, left out (f_esc unknown)
    selected      = candidates & ~faint                                    -> MODELLED WITH CLOUDY

The order matters: a cell that is both "ionised" and "thick" (impossible with these values) or both "thick" and
"off_stromgren" is counted in the first category it meets. Q_MIN must be computed AFTER the other rules and from
the halo's total Q(H), so it needs every star of the halo, not just the selected ones.


8. WHAT TO PASS ON FOR CLOUDY PROCESSING
----------------------------------------
plot_SPHINXHIIs_filtered.py models every cell it is given, so it needs these files in the halo folder:
  gas_cells_filtered.npz   one entry per cell with selected == True:
      cell_index   index of the cell in the full halo extraction (gas_cells.npz order). Names the model folders
                   (cell_0746194, ...), so models made by plot_SPHINXHIIs.py are found and reused
      nH, xHII, TK, metallicity, cell_dx   (required; same units as gas_cells.npz)
      ne, xHeII, xHeIII, cell_pos, cell_l  (optional, not used)
  stars_filtered.npz       every star particle in those cells (star_in_cell == True and host in the selection):
      host_cell    cell_index of the cell that contains the star (step A)
      star_age, star_met, star_minit       (required; same units as stars.npz)
      pos, mass, galaxy_index              (optional, not used)
  halo_info.json           'redshift'
Example: halo 997 with the parameters of section 3 gives 84 cells and 4,559 stars.
For the halo totals (so the halo f_esc can be put together without the full extraction):
  - Q_tot, Q_MIN, and per category (trusted, thick, off-Stromgren over / under, faint, selected):
    number of cells and summed Q_cell
  - for the off-Stromgren cells: sum of Q_cell * exp(-6.3e-18 * N_HI_min), if their f_esc is taken from SPHINX
  - ideally a small per-cell table of ALL star-hosting cells: cell index, Q_cell, N_star, xHII, N_HI_min, crit,
    category. Then the rules can be re-applied with other parameter values without a new extraction.
(plot_SPHINXHIIs.py, by contrast, reads the full gas_cells.npz / stars.npz and applies the rules itself.)

10. NOTES AND CAVEATS
---------------------
1. N_HI_min = nH (1 - x_HII) R_out is the column of a uniform cell, centre to R_out. It is 3x the thin-shell
   column N_target (1 - x_HII) / (4 pi R_out^2) that an older comment in plot_SPHINXHIIs.py described. The
   thresholds N_HI_THIN and N_HI_MAX were chosen with the uniform version: use it.
2. Which cells are lost when the rules are tightened was tested on halo 997 only (631 Cloudy models): the
   off-Stromgren cells have Cloudy f_esc ~0-0.02 and the faint ones ~0.5. Check the category shares of each new
   halo against section 9 before trusting the thresholds.
3. The faint cells' f_esc is unknown (0-1), so the faint cut can change the halo f_esc by at most (1 - Q_FRAC)
   (absolute): 0.02 with Q_FRAC = 0.98.
4. T_ION, Y_He and the BPASS treatment must match plot_SPHINXHIIs.py exactly, or crit, x_S and Q_MIN will differ.
5. CRIT_RANGE in the script is only printed (near-critical cells), not used to select.
